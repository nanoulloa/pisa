# Evaluation

The dataset manifest, scripts and reports live here. Video files go in `eval/raw/`, which is gitignored: clips can show people and are never committed.

## Shot list for real clips

Record on a phone, in normal video mode (not slow motion), at 60 fps if available.

| Clip | What to film |
|---|---|
| `drop` | A tennis ball dropped from 1.5 to 2 m, filmed from the side, with the whole fall and the first bounce in frame. |
| `throw` | The ball thrown in an arc 2 to 3 m wide, with the whole arc in frame. |
| `bounce` | A ball bouncing at least 3 times. |
| `drop-handheld` (optional) | The drop again, holding the phone by hand. |
| `pair-drop` (optional) | Two balls released together from the same height. |

For every clip:

- Keep the phone still on a tripod or propped against something, except in the handheld clip.
- Don't zoom. Keep each clip 3 to 8 seconds long.
- Use a plain background that contrasts with the ball, and good light.
- Put something of known size in the same plane as the motion, such as an A4 sheet (0.297 m) or a 1 m stick on the wall, so the implied-g test (T4) has a scale.
